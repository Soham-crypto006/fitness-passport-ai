To cancel, members must contact the AI agent to submit a cancellation request, with a 30-day notice period applying from the date of contact — during which full facility access continues. Memberships have a minimum 12-month commitment, and cancelling before this term is complete may incur an early cancellation fee ranging from $25 to $325 depending on membership type and how far into the contract the member is. After the 12-month term, no cancellation fee applies but the 30-day notice period still applies.
